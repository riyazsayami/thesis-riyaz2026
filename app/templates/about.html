About SecureCare

Delivering modern, secure and reliable healthcare services.

Who We Are

SecureCare is a healthcare management platform developed as part of an MSc thesis titled Enhancing Security in Containerized CI/CD Pipelines. The application demonstrates how DevSecOps practices can improve the security of modern healthcare systems.

Patients can book appointments, view doctors, and access healthcare information through a secure web application.
